Tournament Overview

Review the competition format and tournament structure.

Tournament Format

01
Preliminary groups

4 groups of up to 8 players. Play 5 FFA rounds; enter cumulative 1st and 2nd manually.

02
Quarterfinals and semi-finals

Four 1v1 quarterfinals are first to 7. Two 1v1 semi-finals are first to 9.

03
Final and placement matches

Championship: first to 11. Third place: first to 9. Quarterfinal losers play a five-round, four-player placement FFA.

Qualification rules
  • Group 1st and 2nd are ordered seeds, not roster positions.
  • Confirmed winners and losers automatically fill downstream rooms.
  • Every correct pick earns 1 point and 1 Lucky Draw ticket. PLACEMENT has no predictions.
  • FFA points are 4/3/2/1 per round. Tied total placings stay pending until an explicit organiser decision.

Room Management

View all rooms, assigned players, room status and published official results.

Lock predictions for an individual room below. Saved picks are retained. Unlocking still requires an open stage, confirmed players and unpublished results; it does not change match status.

Import roster CSV

Upload a CSV with the header room,participantId,username. Use rooms A–D, up to eight participants each. Export spreadsheets as CSV, not XLSX.

Each listed group’s entire roster is replaced in file order; remaining slots stay empty. Groups absent from the file are unchanged. Previewing does not save anything.

Participant IDs stay as text, including leading zeros. Existing IDs retain their player keys. Reopen published groups before changing their rosters.

Initialize or migrate tournament format

Loading this dashboard never changes rooms. Initialize explicitly to create missing rooms and replace incompatible legacy knockout rooms. Group rosters, metadata, results and predictions are retained.

Migration resets incompatible semi-finals, finals and their dependent rooms, and deletes incompatible dependent predictions. Review the confirmation before continuing.
