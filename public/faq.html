FAQ & USER GUIDE

Sign in, save your picks and follow the tournament. Start with the quick steps below, or jump to the question you need.

The Admin dashboard is for tournament organisers. Participants use Predict.

Start in four steps

  1. Open Predict and choose SIGN IN WITH GOOGLE.
  2. Check your Full Name. Edit it and choose Save Full Name if needed.
  3. Choose players in an open room, then choose Save prediction. Repeat for each room you want to predict.
  4. Check your Saved pick, then follow Matches and Leaderboard for published results and points.

Getting started

Do I need an account to use the website?

You can read this guide, Matches and Leaderboard without signing in. To save predictions, open Predict, choose SIGN IN WITH GOOGLE and select your Google account. Use the same account whenever you return.

Does signing in register me as a tournament player?

No. Google sign-in lets you save website predictions; it does not add you to the competitor roster. Ask the organising committee how to register to play and whether separate event registration is required.

Your profile

How do I set up or change my public name?

After signing in, open Full Name on Predict. A valid Google account name is used as your default when a profile is first created; an existing saved name is kept. Edit the field and choose Save Full Name. You can do this even while predictions are closed.

A new name appears on the leaderboard after the organiser's next leaderboard update.

What name can I use, and who can see it?

Use 3–80 characters: letters in any language, numbers, spaces and the name punctuation shown below the field. Include at least one letter or number. Do not enter an email address or other contact details.

Your chosen name is public on the leaderboard and in the Lucky Draw. It does not change your Firebase account, your saves or another participant's picks.

Predictions

How do I submit a prediction?

On Predict, choose two different qualifiers for a group, in either order, or one winner for a quarter-final, semi-final, Grand Final or third-place series. Choose Save prediction for each room.

Selecting a player alone does not save your pick. Check the saved confirmation and the Saved pick shown beneath that room. There are no predictions for the 5th–8th playoff.

Can I change or remove a saved prediction?

Yes, while that room still accepts predictions. For a group, select a chosen player again to deselect them, then choose a replacement. For a series, choose the new winner. Choose Save prediction again to save the change.

Reset clears that room's current selection and removes its saved prediction. It does not restore an earlier version. Closed or locked rooms do not allow changes or resets.

Deadlines

When do predictions close?

Organisers control stage opening and individual room locks; there is no automatic clock-based deadline shown on the site. Follow their announced cutoffs and the message on each Predict room.

You can save only when you are signed in, the stage is open, the room is unlocked, players are confirmed, results are unpublished and the room is not Ended. The Grand Final and third-place match share a stage. Save early rather than waiting for play to begin.

Does Live mean predictions are locked?

No. Live is a match status, not a prediction lock. A room lock, a closed stage, published results or Ended status blocks new picks, changes and resets.

Locking keeps saved picks available to read and score; it does not erase them.

Points & Lucky Draw

How are prediction points calculated?

Each correct selected qualifier or series winner earns 1 point and 1 Lucky Draw entry. A group prediction can earn 0, 1 or 2 points; a series prediction can earn 0 or 1. Qualifier order and match scores do not multiply points.

Unpublished or unresolved results stay pending, not incorrect. Signing in or submitting a pick alone does not currently award points or entries. The organiser must publish results and update the leaderboard before totals change.

Are tournament match scores the same as prediction points?

No. In the current site, quarter-finals are first to 7, semi-finals and the third-place match are first to 9, and the Grand Final is first to 11. Match scores do not multiply prediction points.

The separate 5th–8th playoff has five free-for-all rounds, worth 4, 3, 2 and 1 competition points per round. Those are tournament points, not audience prediction points, and the playoff has no predictions.

Group roster order is not a standing: only the two published qualifiers determine advancement. Match formats shown on Matches follow the current site configuration; confirm any rulebook differences with the organising committee.

How does the Lucky Draw work?

Organisers run the draw. The current website gives one draw entry per correct pick after scores are updated. More entries mean a greater chance, not a guaranteed prize.

Organisers can also add separate manual entries, which do not add leaderboard points. Participants with no entries are not in the draw, and the organiser can exclude previous winners in the current saved draw. Signing in alone does not guarantee eligibility.

Bracket & leaderboard

Where can I follow the bracket and results?

Open Matches and go to All Rooms & Results. Follow Groups A–D into the quarter-finals, semi-finals and Grand Final; third place and the 5th–8th playoff are shown separately.

A1 and A2 mean Group A's published first and second qualifiers, not roster slots. TBD means players are not confirmed yet. Status filters narrow the groups and dim other knockout rooms so bracket connections stay visible.

How do I read the leaderboard?

Open Leaderboard, labelled Ranking in the mobile menu. Picks / Entries counts correct picks; Total Points is the same number. Results must be published and the organiser must update scores before totals change.

Equal totals share a rank, such as 1, 1, 3; names order the tied rows. Find your public name in the table.

Troubleshooting

Google sign-in will not open. What should I try?

Allow pop-ups for this site, check your connection and allow browser storage, then try SIGN IN WITH GOOGLE again. If you closed the Google window, try again.

If the site cannot verify your session, choose RELOAD TO RETRY. To use a different Google account, choose SIGN OUT first.

Why is Save prediction unavailable?

Check the room message. Sign in, wait for saved picks to load, and select exactly two different group players or one series winner. Waiting for confirmed players, a closed stage, a room lock, published results or Ended status prevents saving.

An unchanged saved prediction does not need saving again.

Why are my name, points or saved picks missing?

Check that you are using the same Google account and look for Saved pick on Predict. Unsaved selections are not submissions.

If your public name is Participant, save your Full Name; if it cannot load, choose Retry loading full name. Names and points on Leaderboard wait for the organiser's update. If the bracket changes, affected later-round predictions may be reset—review those rooms before predicting again.

The page will not load or a result looks wrong. Who can help?

Check your connection and reload. Ended — results pending means the room ended but official results are not published yet.

If the problem remains, tell the organising committee the page, room and visible error. Never share passwords or sign-in codes.
